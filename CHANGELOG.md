# Changelog

## 1.2.0 — 2026-09-10

Compatibility with `ggah_mod`'s new cosmological parametrization. The box, the
weights and the network are unchanged; what changed is the conventions the two
packages exchange, and three of those had stopped agreeing.

* **`to_ggah_cosmology` added the wrong neutrino density.** It added
  `Omega_nu`, the 93.14 eV convention, while `ggah_mod` had redefined
  `Omega_cb` to subtract `Omega_nu_matter`, the matter-like part of the
  Fermi-Dirac density, which sits 4.57e-3 above it. The property added must be
  the property the inverse subtracts, and naming two different ones cost
  1.0e-4 of `Omega_cb` at Σmν = 0.3 — straight into `rho_cold`, and so into the
  variance the correction is defined against. It also pushed the
  (Ω_cb = 0.24, Σmν = 0.3) corner back out of the box it was sampled inside.
* **The neutrino hierarchy is now pinned to `degenerate`.** `ggah_mod`'s
  `Cosmology` defaults to a normal ordering, which has no solution below
  0.058993 eV — so an unpinned construction refused 393 of this package's own
  2000 design points, a fifth of the box, while the fiducial 0.06 eV cleared
  the floor by 0.001 and every smoke test passed. `degenerate` is also what the
  shipped weights were fitted under, so pinning it makes that a statement
  rather than a default inherited from elsewhere.
* Conversions now refuse a `ggah_mod` too old to carry either, by probing the
  class rather than reading a version string: both changes landed after that
  package tagged 0.6.0, so no version floor can express the requirement.
* `HmfCorrection` reads back the `params_order` every weights file already
  stamped, and refuses a checkpoint fitted on different columns.
* **Two measured bounds on what the eight parameters cannot represent**, since
  neither can be trained away against a flat simulation suite.
  `target.OMEGA_K_COST` is the curvature cost, `max |Δ ln f|` per unit `|Ω_k|`,
  measured by restoring the `Ω_k(1+z)²` term the emulator's own `E(z)` already
  carries and re-evaluating its carrier at fixed σ: 0.2242 at 200m and 0.8911 at
  virial, so the two files are not interchangeable.
  `target.OMEGA_K_CROSSOVER` is where the correction stops beating the Tinker08
  it replaces, 0.301 and 0.116, derived from the two numbers each weights file
  already records. At the Planck and BAO bound the 200m residual moves from
  0.00518 to 0.00520 against 0.06766 for Tinker08 unchanged, which is why a
  curved cosmology is passed through rather than refused.
  `target.NU_ORDERING_COST` is the neutrino-ordering shift, peaking at 2.2 % of
  the residual at the 0.058993 eV floor and falling to 0.2 % at the box ceiling,
  so the published accuracies are unchanged at both figures they are quoted to.
  The measured ones are regenerated by `docs/make_validity_bounds.py` rather
  than typed, and `OMEGA_K_CROSSOVER` is derived from the coefficient and the
  two figures each weights file already carries, so it cannot drift from them.
  Each carries the limit that belongs with it: what is measured is the carrier's
  response, not the Gaussian process's, because no curved simulations exist.
* **`emu_pk` 2.0.0, and a claim about it that was wrong before and after.**
  `box.py` said this box is "narrower than `emu_pk`'s in three axes" and then
  named two. Measured by mapping the CSST design into `emu_pk`'s coordinates, it
  is narrower and wholly contained on seven of the eight shared axes, and on the
  eighth it *escapes on both sides*: ω_b = Ω_b h² spans 0.0145–0.0382 against
  0.0170–0.0280, putting 30 % of the design outside. That escape is the reason
  generation uses CLASS rather than a network spectrum, which `generate.py`
  already said and `box.py` contradicted.
  `emu_pk` 2.0.0's three further axes — Ω_k and the two neutrino mass ratios —
  are now described as an *absence* rather than a narrowing, because that is the
  distinction that decides what can be done about them: a narrower axis could be
  widened by retraining, an absent one cannot. All of it is asserted in
  `tests/test_box.py` rather than stated in prose.
* **The constants are bound to the measurement that produced them.**
  `docs/make_validity_bounds.py` writes an archive and a human copies rounded
  numbers out of it, which is a hand step in the middle of a measured chain.
  `tests/test_target.py` now checks every constant against
  `docs/data/validity_bounds.npz`. It immediately earned its place: the script
  had come to mean the curvature coefficient over its whole sweep, and
  extending that sweep to |Ω_k| = 0.3 to measure the fall diluted the number
  with its own tail, returning 0.2112 where the constant says 0.2242. The
  constant was right. The coefficient is the small-curvature one, meaned below
  |Ω_k| = 0.05, because that is the regime it is quoted in; averaging over the
  tail gives a number that is wrong where it is used and right only on average.
* **Both curvature crossings are measured, not extrapolated.** The sweep now
  runs to |Ω_k| = 0.45, which brackets both: the 200m correction still beats
  Tinker08 at 0.35 and no longer does at 0.40, putting the crossing at 0.3715
  against the linear law's 0.3009. Virial's sits at 0.1210 against 0.1162. The
  law errs early on both and by more where it reaches further, which is what a
  falling coefficient should do; `target.py` asserts that direction at import,
  because refusing late would recommend a correction already worse than the
  carrier while every number still looked reasonable.
* **The `ggah_mod` seam is pinned from this side.** That package restates the
  measured curvature constants in its own capability table, and two copies of a
  number drift silently while every value stays plausible. `tests/test_target.py`
  now checks its table against `target.OMEGA_K_COST` and its three companions
  field by field, checks both recalibrations are still registered and still
  cosmology-dependent, and checks the two entry points agree to 1e-12.
* The test suite no longer skips its CSST half silently. `importorskip` reached
  `CEmulator` before the shim that makes `CEmulator` importable, turning a
  broken dependency into thirteen skips and hiding two genuine failures.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-08-29

First public release.

* Recalibrated Tinker08 multiplicity function at two halo definitions,
  `RockstarM200m` and `RockstarMvir`, each fitted on 2000 CSST cosmologies over
  twelve redshifts and 456 526 rows inside ν ∈ [0.5, 3].
  Residual in ln f, on cosmologies held out entirely: 7.00 % → 0.52 % at 200m,
  10.92 % → 0.54 % at virial.
* Pure-JAX inference path: `jax.grad`, `jax.jit` and `jax.vmap` all pass through
  the cosmology. Two runtime dependencies, numpy and JAX.
* `target.nu_covered(z)` records the peak heights the training set actually
  spans, which narrows with redshift as growth pushes σ down.
* Both weight files are float64 and carry their own provenance — mass
  definition, sample size, held-out split and residual.
